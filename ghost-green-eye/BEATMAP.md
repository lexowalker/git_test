## Through-line motifs
- **The triad**: SELF (circle) — VALUED (diamond) — RIVAL (triangle). Solid bond, dashed approach, and a zig-zag tension line that turns green. It first crystallises out of the stag scene (0:13), then reappears in the bar, Othello, the AI, and finally on its own.
- **The green eye**: the only saturated green in the film. It's reserved for jealousy: the stag's inner glow, your iris, the bar's neon eye, the ink monster, the AI's flicker, the crowd of minds, the closing giant eye.
- **The night world**: the same Moebius plateau (starfield, crystal massif, mesas) shows up through the bar window, under the AI crystals, and in the finale.
- **Colour-coded people**: teal = you, coral = her, ochre = rival. Diagrams reuse these colours, so the abstract scenes stay legible.
